\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Protocol before architecture.}
If a number looks surprisingly good, suspect split leakage and metric
pooling before celebrating an architectural gain
\cite{corley_2024_reality}.

\paragraph{Loss vs.\ calibration.}
BCE+Dice is the right overlap objective under 2--5\% prevalence and
the wrong calibration objective \cite{mehrtash_2020_calibration}.
Temperature scaling on shift data is the cheap, default remedy
\cite{ovadia_2019_shift}; a calibration-aware auxiliary term is the
escalation path.

\paragraph{Deferral, not autonomy.}
Pair-level AURC answers ``which pairs should a human see.'' Pixel-
fragmented deferral maps are the wrong UX for this product.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Free-tier GPUs, $n=3$ seeds, authorised video set may not land
(escape hatch: report as paired-image CD only). WHU-CD is not used as
a comparable leaderboard number given documented split leakage.
