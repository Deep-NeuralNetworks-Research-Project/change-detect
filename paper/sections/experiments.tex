\label{sec:experiments}

We evaluate on SYSU-CD, LEVIR-CD (256$\times$256 non-overlapping
crops, 7{,}120 / 1{,}024 / 2{,}048), and PCD/TSUNAMI, with
scene-disjoint splits. Principal models use seeds $\{0,1,2\}$.
Baselines: RGB/SSIM, FC-Siam-Diff \cite{daudt_2018_fcsiam}, Siamese
ResNet-18. Proposed: encoder + signed fusion + bounded alignment +
confidence head + pair-order consistency (see P4). Ablations follow
the B-grid in the research brief (fusion, alignment, gate, pair-order).
Swap-consistency is measured on \emph{every} model, including those
not trained with the consistency loss (B7).
