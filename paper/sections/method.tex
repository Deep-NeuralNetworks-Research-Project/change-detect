\subsection{Evaluation protocol}
\label{sec:method-eval}

\paragraph{Overlap.}
Primary: aggregate changed-class precision, recall, F1, and IoU,
accumulated over every valid pixel of the test set. Secondary:
per-image mF1, excluding true-negative-only images (reported instead
as image-level false-alert rate). Changed-pixel ratio $\pi$ is stated
for every split and every nuisance stratum. The decision threshold is
selected on validation scenes only, and we record whether it is
F1-optimal or precision-constrained.

\paragraph{Boundary and regions.}
Boundary IoU \cite{cheng_2021_boundaryiou} with
\texttt{dilation\_ratio}=0.02; HD95 and average surface distance with
empty-mask guards. Connected components use 8-connectivity. We sweep
minimum region size $k\in\{1,5,20,50\}$ rather than hiding a single
$k$. Object-level F1 uses greedy IoU matching at $\tau=0.5$.

\paragraph{Calibration and deferral.}
Class-wise and foreground-restricted ECE with equal-mass bins; Brier
(with Murphy reliability) and NLL. Temperature $T$ is fitted on a
shift-representative validation slice \cite{ovadia_2019_shift}.
Pair-level scores: max-softmax-response aggregate (baseline) and
Soft-Dice-Confidence \cite{gauthier_2024_sdc}. We report AURC and
E-AURC and pick a deferral operating point on validation.

\paragraph{Nuisance suite.}
Corruptions (brightness/gamma, ASC-CDL, blur, JPEG, H.264/HEVC
round-trip, shadow, viewpoint, occlusion) are applied to exactly one
frame, both directions. Primary robustness scalar: F1 retention vs.\
clean.

\paragraph{Efficiency.}
Pair-input FLOPs via \texttt{torch.utils.flop\_counter} (true FLOPs;
MACs reported as FLOPs$/2$). Latency: warm-up excluded, CUDA events,
median + IQR, GPU model logged every run. Peak memory:
\texttt{max\_memory\_reserved} headline.

\paragraph{Seeds.}
$\ge 3$ seeds for principal models. We report mean $\pm$ std and
median [min, max], plus bootstrap CIs over the test set. We do not
run significance tests at $n=3$.
