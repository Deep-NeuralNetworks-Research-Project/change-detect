\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Change detection.}
Fully convolutional Siamese networks \cite{daudt_2018_fcsiam} remain
the mandatory baseline. A well-tuned plain U-Net is competitive with
much of the ``SOTA'' once the evaluation protocol is held fixed
\cite{corley_2024_reality}; we therefore treat baseline recipes as
seriously as the proposed model.

\paragraph{Boundary quality.}
Region IoU is insensitive to boundary error on large objects.
Boundary IoU \cite{cheng_2021_boundaryiou} is scale-balanced and is
our primary boundary metric (\texttt{dilation\_ratio}=0.02). HD95 is
reported as a secondary, pixel-interpretable number.

\paragraph{Calibration and selective prediction.}
Guo et al.\ \cite{guo_2017_calibration} established temperature
scaling; Nixon et al.\ recommend equal-mass bins; Ovadia et al.\
\cite{ovadia_2019_shift} showed that in-distribution $T$ does not
transfer under shift. Pair-level deferral follows the risk--coverage
framework of Geifman \& El-Yaniv, with Soft Dice Confidence
\cite{gauthier_2024_sdc} as the primary ranking score and
max-softmax-response as the baseline. Dice-induced overconfidence is
documented by Mehrtash et al.\ \cite{mehrtash_2020_calibration} and
Yeung et al.\ \cite{yeung_2023_dscpp}; Focal loss, by contrast,
improves calibration \cite{mukhoti_2020_focal}.

\paragraph{Robustness.}
ImageNet-C \cite{hendrycks_2019_imagenetc} supplies the severity
philosophy; we re-tune the schedule for paired RGB frames and apply
each corruption to \emph{one} frame of the pair only.
